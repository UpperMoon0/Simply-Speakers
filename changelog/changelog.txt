=====================================================
  Simply Speakers - Changelog
=====================================================

-----------------------------------------------------
  Version 0.7.9
-----------------------------------------------------

 Added
  - Server Config Synchronization: Server configs (speaker range,
    upload availability, and upload limits) now synchronize on join
    and hot reload, then restore client values after disconnecting.
  - Overlapping Speaker Audio Blending: Speakers and proxies sharing
    the same ID blend into a single synchronized virtual multi-point
    emitter, eliminating echoes, comb filtering, and volume doubling.
  - Incremental MP3/WAV decoding, positional downmixing, bounded client
    caching, and duration-aware playback.

 Changed
  - Speaker state is authoritative, dimension-safe, and synchronized
    between clients, including linked networks and standalone speakers.
  - Linked mains share playback while keeping independent redstone and
    emitter lifecycles; chunk unloads preserve their state.
  - Listener scans are throttled and always obey the live server range.
  - Audio upload/download workers and temporary resources are scoped to
    each world and cleaned up safely.

 Fixed
  - Fixed multi-main removal, relinking, restart, and power-off behavior.
  - Fixed stale loop, selection, GUI, pending-download, stop, and delete
    state on clients.
  - Fixed valid MP3 validation, temporary upload validation, natural EOF,
    and standalone ghost playback after audio deletion.
  - Fixed legacy standalone migration, persistent IDs, registry backup,
    corruption recovery, and chunk-unload state loss.

 Security
  - Added server-side packet access/range/value validation, upload size
    and content checks, transfer limits/timeouts, and authorized audio
    downloads.

-----------------------------------------------------
  Version 0.7.8
-----------------------------------------------------

 Fixed
  - Looping audio now resumes at the correct position after
    leaving a speaker's range and returning.
  - Short audio clips now continue looping indefinitely
    instead of stopping after only a few repetitions.
  - Fixed an OpenAL buffer cleanup race that caused permanent
    buffer underruns during loop playback.
  - Loop transitions now finish playing all queued audio,
    preventing clipped or shortened repetitions.
