# Simply Speakers 0.7.8

## Fixed

- Fixed a game startup compatibility issue when Simply Speakers is installed alongside mods that bundle the same MP3 audio library, including Ad Astra.
- The compatibility fix applies to the Fabric and Forge builds for Minecraft 1.20.1 and the Fabric and NeoForge builds for Minecraft 1.21.1.
- Fixed active audio continuing after a speaker was broken or its ID was changed.
- Fixed newly linked proxy speakers failing to join audio that was already playing.
