# Simply Speakers 0.7.9

## Added

- **Server Config Synchronization**:
  - Automatically syncs server-side configuration (`speaker_range`, `disable_upload`, `max_upload_size`) to connected clients upon joining multiplayer and dedicated servers.
  - Fixes speaker settings UI to respect the active server-side max range limit rather than local client config.
  - Automatically restores client-local configuration upon disconnecting or quitting a world.

- **Overlapping Speaker Audio Blending (Virtual Multi-Point Emitter)**:
  - Linked speakers (main and proxy speakers sharing the same Speaker ID) in overlapping audible areas now play through a single unified audio stream.
  - Eliminates phase cancellation, comb filtering, and audio desynchronization artifacts caused by multiple concurrent decoders.
  - Implements a dynamic gain-weighted spatial centroid that calculates the optimal 3D emitter position and peak gain envelope in real time as the player moves between speakers.
  - Prevents unnatural volume doubling in overlapping broadcast zones while maintaining full volume coverage across the entire speaker network.
